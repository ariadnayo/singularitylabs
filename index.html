Drug development is a probability problem.

Most drug programs fail. What one is worth depends on how likely it is to clear each stage. Singularity Labs is building a model that estimates those odds and prices them.

A preview of the model’s output

For a single program, the model estimates how strong the evidence is, the odds of clearing each stage, and what those odds are worth. The values below are invented.

How we work

  • Every figure links back to its source.

    Our data layer records where each value came from and when it was retrieved. It starts with public trial registries.

  • We show uncertainty.

    Estimates carry ranges, and cases with little evidence behind them are marked as such.

  • We would rather leave a record unlabeled than guess.

    When a trial outcome doesn’t clearly match a known endpoint, our pipeline leaves it unclassified and says why. It has been checked against real registry records.
